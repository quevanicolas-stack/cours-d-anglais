Leçon 2
Une tâche trop souvent négligée

Confirm It in Writing

1

“Just confirming our call tomorrow at 3pm your time — that’s 6pm here in Réunion.”

2

“Looking forward to our call — I’ll send the video link 10 minutes before.”

3

“Quick reminder: our call is set for [time], [timezone] — let me know if that still works.”

Fuseaux horaires

your time my time [X hours] ahead of [X hours] behind Does [time] work on your end?

Phone-Specific Phrases

Sans l’image, plus de verbalisation

1

“Just to confirm, is this [Name]?”

2

“I’m calling regarding…”

3

“Sorry, you’re breaking up a little — could you say that again?”

4

“Let me just note that down — could you spell that for me?”

5

“I’ll follow up with an email to confirm everything we discussed.”
